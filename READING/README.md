# 📚📚 IELTS READING

## There will be three passages.

## Question Types

For any passage, the following types of questions will be asked.

### Ordered

1.  Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ)
    
2.  True / False / Not Given
    
3.  Yes / No / Not Given
    
4.  Matching Sentence Endings
    
5.  Sentence Completion
    
6.  Summary Completion
    
7.  Note Completion
    
8.  Table Completion
    
9.  Flow-chart Completion
    
10.  Short-answer Questions
    

### Unordered

1.  Matching Headings
    
2.  Matching Information
    
3.  Matching Features / Names
    
4.  Diagram Label Completion
    

**Ordered:** These questions' answers will appear in order in the texts, like one after another sequentially.

**Unordered:** These questions' answers will appear in random order in the texts.

# 🎯🎯 Perfect Plan for Reading Test Answering

## For any passage:

(First, look 👀👀👀👀 at the questions for 30 seconds, to understand what type of questions they are and what the question is asking. Briefly look at these. Just to keep a mind map, like if the questions are asking about a person and their quote in text or speech or something, so that you know what to mark when reading the passage.)

(📖📖📖📖📖📖📖 Now read through the passage very first (skimming). Spend 3–5 minutes max; for passage 3, you may need 6 minutes. You should highlight important things like person names, place names, passage context, etc. You will get these contexts (what to highlight) while you read the questions initially for 30 seconds.)

(➡️➡️➡️➡️➡️➡️➡️ Answer **ORDERED** questions first. Not "True / False / Not Given or Yes / No / Not Given".)

While looking for these answers, you will have a much better understanding of the text or passage. Like what it is about, what each paragraph talks about.

Then,

(➡️➡️➡️➡️➡️➡️➡️ Answer **TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN** or **YES / NO / NOT GIVEN** questions.)

Then,

(🔀🔀🔀🔀🔀 Answer **UNORDERED** questions.)

## Time Management ⏰⏰⏰⏰⏰

### 🟢 Passage 1: Spend max 15 minutes (now you have 45 minutes left)

### 🟠 Passage 2: Spend max 20 minutes (now you have 25 minutes left for passage 3)

### 🔴 Passage 3: Spend 25 minutes.

## Most Difficult Question Types

**1. TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN:**

To answer these questions, forget any previous/prior knowledge you have about the topic or passage, as you will confuse them with the question answer. You have to answer only based on the text provided in the passage. Now, how to answer........

Now, for TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN, you are dealing with **facts** about the passage.

**After paraphrasing the question, understand its meaning and find information about it in the text/passage.**

**TRUE:** After paraphrasing, if the meaning/fact matches the paragraph's, that section's meaning, then TRUE.

**FALSE:** After paraphrasing, if the meaning/fact **clearly** opposes the paragraph's, that section's meaning, then it's FALSE.

**NOT GIVEN:** After paraphrasing, if sufficient information is not available to us to **clearly** say it is TRUE or FALSE, then the answer is NOT GIVEN.

**1. YES / NO / NOT GIVEN:**

To answer these questions, forget any previous/prior knowledge you have about the topic or passage, as you will confuse them with the question answer. You have to answer only based on the text provided in the passage. Now, how to answer........

Now, for YES / NO / NOT GIVEN, you are dealing with **the opinion of the writer, not anyone else's, even though their speech is present in the text.**

**After paraphrasing the question, understand its meaning and find information about it in the text/passage.**

**YES:** After paraphrasing, if the meaning/opinion matches the writer's, that section's meaning, then it is YES.

**FALSE:** After paraphrasing, if the meaning/opinion **clearly** opposes or contradicts the writer's, that section's meaning, then it is FALSE.

**NOT GIVEN:** After paraphrasing, if sufficient information is not available to us to **clearly** say the writer meant this, then the answer is NOT GIVEN.
