# IELTS-INSTANT-PREP

In this repository, I discussed how to approach the IELTS exam with minimal preparation time, particularly the Listening and Reading modules, including the order in which to answer questions and how to manage time effectively.

I believe it’s all about having a mind map for approaching the exam. I don’t believe in long, tedious practice sessions or following a rigid study plan. Instead, I believe in simply practicing with a clear strategy.

You create a mind map, follow it, practice with it, and refine it as you improve. Ultimately, you develop a clear, well-defined strategy and stick to it.
