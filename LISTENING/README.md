# 🇬🇧🎧 IELTS Listening

 ## Part 1

 A form will be given. It may be a booking form, student registration form, travelling form, or something like that.

 ## Part 2

 It will involve a speaker giving directions or pointers. It may be about the locations of something or visiting some historical places or a campus.

 Now, the questions may be only MCQ + (MCQ with multiple answers).

 Or it may be MCQ + labelling a MAP.

Or MCQ + matching sentences.

 ## Part 3

 It will involve a conversation between two academics, students, or a student and teacher.

 Questions will involve MCQ + (MCQ with multiple answers).
 
 Or MCQ + matching sentences.

 ## Part 4

 It will be a presentation regarding a particular subject, research, or topic. Questions will be about filling in the blanks.

 # 🎯🎯 Perfect Plan for Listening Test Answering

 ## 🟢 Part 1

 (👂👂 Listening to the instruction first)

 Check the word count for the answer. Is it one word only, or two words, or one word and/or a number, etc.?

 (⏰⏰ You will be given about 30 sec to check the questions 1–5 or 1–6.)

 (👀👀 Now you look at the questions carefully and be ready to answer. This is the only time you will look at the question after you have heard the instructions)

 Answer as you hear the audio 👂👂.

 Then,

 (⏰⏰ You will be given about 30 sec to check the questions 6–10 or 7–10.)

 (👀👀 Now you look at the questions carefully and be ready to answer.)

 Answer as you hear the audio 👂👂.

 (⏰⏰ You will be given about 30–60 sec to check all the answers. ⚠️🚨 Don't waste a second here. Immediately move to Part 2 and save this time.)

 ## 🟡 Part 2

 (🧠✨ You have saved 30–60 seconds from the previous section.)

 (👀👀 Immediately you look at the questions 11–16 / 11–15 carefully, if possible, a bit 17–20 / 16–20 as well and be mentally prepared, whether it is a map or matching sentences.)

 (👂👂 Then the instruction will start. Start listening to the instructions. Don't look at the questions during the instructions.)

 (⏰⏰ You will be given about 30 sec to check the questions 11–15 or 11–16.)

 (👀👀 Now you look at the questions 11–15 or 11–16's options, and then again you look at the first two questions (11–12) options, and keep them in immediate memory. Be ready to hear the audio.)

 Answer as you hear the audio 👂👂.

 (⏰⏰ You will be given about 30 sec to check the questions 16–20 or 17–20.)

 Answer as you hear the audio 👂👂.

 (⏰⏰ You will be given about 30 sec to check all the answers. ⚠️🚨 Don't waste a second here. Immediately move to Part 3 and save this time.)

 ## 🟠 Part 3

 (🧠✨ You have saved 30 seconds from the previous section.)

 (👀👀 Immediately you look at the questions 21–26 / 21–25 carefully, if possible, a bit 27–30 / 26–30 as well and be mentally prepared whether it is matching sentences or just MCQ with multiple options.)

 (👂👂 Then the instruction will start. Start listening to the instructions. Don't look at the questions during the instructions.)

 (⏰⏰ You will be given about 30 sec to check the questions 21–25 or 21–26.)

 (👀👀 Now you look at the questions 21–25 or 21–26's options, and then again you look at the first two questions (21–22) options, and keep them in immediate memory. Be ready to hear the audio.)

 Answer as you hear the audio 👂👂.

 (⏰⏰ You will be given about 30 sec to check the questions 26–30 or 27–30.)

 Answer as you hear the audio 👂👂.

 (⏰⏰ You will be given about 30 sec to check all the answers. ⚠️🚨 Don't waste a second here. Immediately move to Part 4 and save this time.)

 **(⚠️🚨⚠️🚨⚠️🚨 This is the most important part. If you don't save these 30 seconds here, you will not be able to look at all 31–40 questions.)**

 ## 🔴 Part 4

 (🧠✨ You have saved 30 seconds from the previous section.)

 (👀👀 Immediately you look at the questions 31–36. As it will be a presentation or article with filling in the blanks, you may need to read the entire sentences along with questions, as word form matters (whether it is noun, verb, adj., etc.).)

 (👂👂 Then the instruction will start. Start listening to the instructions. Don't look at the questions during the instructions.)

 (⏰⏰ You will be given about 30 sec.)

 (👀👀 Immediately you look at the questions 36–40. Then again questions 31–33, along with full sentences, and predict the form of the word (whether it is noun, verb, adj., etc.). Be ready for the first three questions by keeping them in immediate memory.)

 Answer as you hear the audio 👂👂.

 (⏰⏰⏰⏰⏰⏰ Finally, you will be given about 2 minutes to check all the answers of all sections. Check for any spelling mistakes with this time in Part 1, 2, 3, 4. Nothing else.)

 That's it, you got 9.0.

 # Things to Practice More

 ## Part 2 Labelling Map Questions

 They can be tricky. From your starting point, you need to be familiar with map language. Practice it properly.

 ## Part 2 and 3 Matching Sentences Questions

 Practice them properly. You need to read both the incomplete sentences and all their unmatched halves as well.
